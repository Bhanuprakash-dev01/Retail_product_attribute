# Headphones specification guide

## Battery life
- Product battery life may be listed in hours.
- Typical wireless headphones deliver between 20 and 40 hours of playback.
- Values outside this range may need review.

## Connectivity
- Bluetooth is the expected wireless standard for consumer headphones.
- Wired connectivity may be reported as an alternate option.

## Weight
- Consumer wireless headphones usually range from 150g to 350g.
- Values outside this range should be reviewed for accuracy.
